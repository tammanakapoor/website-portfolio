Daily Café

A room for quiet days.

  • Website
  • Illustration
  • Sound
visit café ↗

One button, a hand-drawn room, and ninety-six instrumental tracks ordered so the day rises to midday and softens towards closing. Made for people who miss the noise of a café.

I missed the sound of a café more than the coffee. A playlist alone wasn’t quite it. I wanted the cups, the low hum of people, and the feeling that someone else was looking after the room.

the story, from the beginning ↓

01 / the first thought

It started with a little “what if…”

Working from home could feel too quiet. So I started with a small question: could a webpage feel like somewhere you could settle in? No account to make, no room to choose. Just arrive, press one button, and stay a while.

What if a webpage could feel like a place?
  • one room, one button
  • draw the mood before the interface
  • let the sound look after itself
02 / on the worktable

From a thought to a first try.

I drew the room first: the barista, tiled counter, menu board and plants. That drawing decided the mood. Then I built the sound in layers—a riverside café, an espresso machine fading in later, and music moving gently through the day.

The drawing came first. Everything else had to belong in this room.

03 / the crossed-out bits

Try. Notice. Try again.

  1. a very quiet room↳ people, cups, a little movement

    The first recording felt empty.

  2. a door bell↳ let the room hum

    It was the one thing everyone noticed.

  3. songs with vocals↳ instrumentals only

    Words pulled people away from their work.

04 / out in the world

The thing it became.

One button, a hand-drawn room, and 96 credited instrumental tracks by 59 artists. Tracks crossfade, the ambience keeps going, and the day rises towards midday before softening again. A small place to leave open while doing something else.

one little idea, later96 tracksone small button

05 / notes to keep

A few things for the next page.

  • Licensing shapes what a project can become.
  • Restraint is something people can feel.
  • Sort with measurements. Listen with people.

Keep listening. The smallest sound can change how a whole room feels.

still learning. still making. ♡
