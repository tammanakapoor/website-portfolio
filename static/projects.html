No. 01 / ProjectLive

Daily Café

A quiet café-style webpage. You arrive, press one button, and music starts with the sound of a coffee shop underneath it.

One button, a hand-drawn room, and ninety-six instrumental tracks ordered so the day rises to midday and softens towards closing. Made for people who miss the noise of a café.

No. 02 / ProjectPublic beta

FloraQuest

A friendly guessing game that turns plant knowledge into a trail of clues, branches and discoveries.

Three small guessing games and a field journal, built to test whether a guess made before the answer makes the answer stick. 325 reviewed plants, in public beta and still changing.

  • Learning game
  • Product design
  • UX research
No. 03 / ProjectTested in class

Making Energy Changes Visible

An interactive secondary chemistry learning experience exploring exothermic and endothermic reactions, energy profiles, activation energy and catalysts.

Three H5P experiences, built in Lumi, that move learners from a thermometer reading to an energy-profile diagram to a catalyst’s pathway. Tested with Year 10 and 11 students, and reshaped by what they found hard.

  • Learning design
  • H5P · Lumi
  • Chemistry
