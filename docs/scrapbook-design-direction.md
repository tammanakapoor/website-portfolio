# Scrapbook design direction

## References supplied by the owner

- https://anti-portfolio.framer.website/ — inspected the opening spread and Printer Prediction spread. Physical binder construction, large metal rings, layered cutout objects, dot/grid paper, handwritten process notes, a visual project index and turning project spreads.
- https://taliahhh.com/ — inspected the opening spread and its next spread. Worn leather binder, generous white pages, bold hand-lettered colour accents, clear persistent navigation, visible page-turn arrows, personal photographs and interactive objects such as a television and record. The next spread includes a draggable testimonial image and pointer interaction prompts.

The owner has identified both as examples of the experience they want. They have offered additional inspiration and images. A palette change has not been selected, and no new personal photographs have been supplied in this conversation.

## Proposed adaptation for Tammana

- Keep the journal as the main navigation and storytelling space, with a larger open-book presentation and more convincing binding, paper edges and material details.
- Combine the first reference's personal process collages with the second reference's generous spacing, visible navigation and occasional object interaction.
- Preserve the established brown leather, warm paper, botanical imagery and personal voice as the working direction until the owner chooses otherwise. Do not copy either reference's branding, text, photographs or project content.
- Bring project stories into the journal's page sequence while retaining direct links and standalone project URLs. Keep each spread readable and give each project a distinct visual story using its actual assets.
- Keep the new high-contrast cutout story and live-project links. Daily Café, FloraQuest and the chemistry experiences have live destinations; Fieldnotes remains a private prototype.
- Use real sketches, project screenshots, embroidery and walking photographs already in the repository. Additional owner portraits could support a personal photo strip; rough project notes and sketches could enrich the process spreads.
- Keep page controls obvious and keyboard accessible. Preserve phone layouts, reduced-motion support and selectable text.

This document records reference research and a proposed direction. It does not mark the broader scrapbook redesign as implemented or published.
