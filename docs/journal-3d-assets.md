# Journal cover materials

The cover is a Three.js model with a hinged leather cover, 32 paper layers, a brass clip, an elastic cord, an amber locket, a pearl, stone beads, a stitched leather star, and a fountain pen. Pointer movement changes its angle and the charms sway. Existing personal photographs are used for the cover's photo strip. A native HTML opening button and the original cover provide keyboard access and a WebGL fallback.

Two raster materials were created with the built-in image_gen tool. The leather was converted from PNG to JPEG for delivery; the lace retains its generated alpha channel. The cloth and lettered paper label are code-authored canvas textures.

## Leather prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: a seamless PBR base-color texture for a 3D vintage leather journal cover. Flat orthographic macro photograph of a single uninterrupted square of very dark warm espresso-brown full-grain leather, with a subtle oxblood undertone, beautifully worn and supple, fine natural irregular pores and tiny creases, low contrast, restrained patina. Uniform neutral diffuse lighting, no directional shadow, no specular highlights baked into the texture, no perspective. Edge-to-edge leather, seamless tileable edges. No notebook, no seams, no hardware, no paper, no border, no lettering or watermark. Photographic material scan, tactile and realistic, not procedural-looking noise.

## Lace prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: a transparent photographic cutout of an antique ivory cotton lace ribbon tied in a loose little bow, intended as a realistic trim on the front of a dark leather journal. Viewed exactly from directly above, one bow only, approximately square overall silhouette: two softly folded loops at the top and two long, gently curling ribbon tails hanging downward, about three times the knot's height. Fine floral crochet lace with open holes, actual threads, delicate scalloped edges, cream aged cotton, subtle real fabric folds and tiny self-shadows. Soft neutral daylight from upper left. The whole bow and tails visible with generous padding. Actual transparent alpha background including lace holes. No surface underneath, no hands, no notebook, no jewelry, no text, no border. Photorealistic studio product cutout, not an illustration.
